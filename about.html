Our story

About Euphoria ASU

"We enlighten your soul." Euphoria ASU is the entertainment club at Ain Shams University — started by students who wanted campus life to include real trips, outings, and activities, not just lectures and labs.

Mission

What we're trying to build.

We organize trips, outings, and on-campus activities that give students a reason to leave the library, meet people outside their own faculty, and actually enjoy their time at university.

01

Open to everyone

Every faculty, every year. You don't need experience to apply — just curiosity.

02

Student-run, top to bottom

Every trip, outing, and activity is planned and run by the members themselves.

03

Built to last

We plan for the club to outlive any one member — with handovers built in every year.
